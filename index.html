🌿 MindEase

A quiet place to talk, write and breathe.

A Digital Mental Health and Psychological Support System for college students. Reach your counselling cell, keep a private diary and find calm, at any hour and with the option to stay anonymous.

Support tickets

Choose your problem and describe it. Your ticket goes only to a counsellor of the matching section, and you can stay anonymous.

Digital diary

Write freely and tag your mood. Entries are visible only to you, and a weekly chart shows how you have been feeling.

Calm sound and SOS

Switch on calming ambient sound any time, and reach the emergency helpline with one tap.

Wellness check

Short quizzes, including the standard PHQ-2 and GAD-7, show what kind of problem you are facing and point you to the right counsellor.

Relaxation section

Pick a book, movie or game and get a recommendation that suits how you feel.

How it works

1

Create your profile

Log in or sign up as a student or a counsellor, then tell us a little about yourself. Counsellors choose the sections they handle.

2

Reach out your way

Raise a support ticket, write in your diary, chat with the companion or take a moment to breathe.

3

Get the right support

Your ticket reaches a counsellor trained for that problem, and serious cases are flagged for a professional counsellor.

Why it matters

Many students struggle in silence because of stigma or because help is hard to reach. MindEase supports SDG 3 (good health and well-being) by offering early, private support and stress management, and SDG 10 (reduced inequalities) by giving every student equal, stigma-free access. It is built for Smart India Hackathon 2025 problem statement SIH25092.
